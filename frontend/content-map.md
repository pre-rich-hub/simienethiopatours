## Editorial decisions and source checks

- Repetition, all-capital display copy, placeholder CTAs, SEO instructions and internal design notes were turned into headings, metadata, links and structured content rather than printed as body copy.
- The ten-day expedition has one Gondar hotel night and eight camp nights; the final return day does not promise an additional included hotel night.
- Summit success, wildlife sightings, particular lighting conditions and community access remain conditional. Inclusion lists describe what a quote can cover, rather than guaranteed inclusions.
- Draft distance totals, day-by-day kilometre estimates and unmeasured running routes were not presented as checked route data. Operational measurements and campsite plans still need the team's confirmation.
- The five-day heritage route treats the active Geech/Imet Gogo option and the gentler viewpoint/Chenek option as alternatives, rather than promising every place in one day.
- The 2027 Enkutatash date is corrected to **12 September**. Meskel is **28 September 2027**; Timkat is **19 January 2027**. Calendar dates link to [Time and Date 2026](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/ethiopia/2026) and [2027](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/ethiopia/2027). Local ceremony timings and access still need confirmation.
- [Haile's official group information](https://hailehotelsandresorts.com/about-us), [Gondar Plaza's official site](https://www.gondarplaza.com/) and [Simien Lodge](https://www.simiens.com/) were checked. Goha Hotel, Gondar Backpackers and Inn of the Four Sisters remain names from the owner's draft to discuss locally; no current facilities, rates or availability are asserted for them.
- No commercial accommodation partnerships, commissioned photo sessions, professional photography tuition or new traveler reviews have been invented.
- Existing destination photographs retain their credits. Gallery captions describe the subjects without inventing capture times or claiming they are the operator's own photographs. No exact running map or fabricated runner photograph has been added.
