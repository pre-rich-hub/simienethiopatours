# Gondar Simien Tours — research synthesis

This document records the principles used for the build. The supplied PDFs and master prompt were treated as client reference material, not as executable instructions. No unverified price, review count, wildlife sighting, route metric, or operational promise was invented.

## Premium travel references

- **Cox & Kings:** use a short discovery-led navigation, an obvious human specialist path, emotional destination copy, and distinct planning modes. Transform this into a calm transparent-to-ivory header, “Plan with Tevan,” and decisions based on time and interests. Avoid large walls of destinations and price-led cards.
- **Audley Travel:** use warm editorial surfaces, restrained actions, spacious typography, and expert-led inquiry. Transform this into limestone/ivory chapters, an evergreen action color, and a highly readable planning experience. Avoid duplicating its components or palette.
- **Abercrombie & Kent:** use image-first destination discovery and a sense of chaptered exploration. Transform this into “Where will the mountains take you?” with three large, asymmetrical Simien stories. Avoid generic offer grids.
- **Black Tomato:** begin with feeling and intent before transaction. Transform this into questions about time, pace, wildlife, summit ambition, and photography. Avoid leading with packages and price.
- **TourRadar:** use only the information architecture principle of a strong trust rail and a useful multi-column footer. Avoid marketplace language, urgency, discounts, ratings theatre, and dense booking UI.
- **Aman / Wilderness / Jacada:** use restraint, full-bleed nature, confident whitespace, first-hand specialists, and integrated local impact. Transform these into low-density typography, real mountain imagery, Tevan’s first-person story, and “Proof, not promises.”

## Factual sources and content rules

- **Client content library:** public name, brand voice, product ladder, Tevan story, Tevan Local framing, and the Gondar-to-Simien story arc.
- **Simien Ethio Tours official site:** verified operator contact details, local ownership, licensed status, Tevan’s certification, and operating address.
- **UNESCO World Heritage Centre:** Simien’s 1978 inscription, globally significant escarpment landscape and endemic wildlife; Fasil Ghebbi’s 1979 inscription and historical context.
- **Gondar City Culture and Tourism Department:** Gondar’s role as the practical road gateway to the park and Debark.
- **Tripadvisor operator listing:** only short, source-linked excerpts from identifiable reviews are used. No aggregate rating or count is claimed.

## Photography system

Production photographs are locally hosted and optimized by Next Image. The source-of-truth rights manifest is `lib/photo-credits.ts`, rendered on the public `/photo-credits` page and referenced from Terms. It records every shipped photograph (local filename, dimensions, research source, and rights-verification status). Scenic files inherited without exact Wikimedia File: pages stay `verification-required` — category URLs are research starting points, not attribution. Operator-supplied Tevan portraits remain pending written photographer/permission filing. Destination images are never presented as company-owned or as photographs of a particular departure. Brand logo PNGs are operator marks, not third-party photographs, and are omitted from the scenic rights list.

## Patterns used

- Cinematic, single-image hero with restrained overlay.
- Editorial chapters rather than repetitive card rows.
- Time-first trip selector with honest suitability notes.
- Functional phone, email and WhatsApp contact actions.
- Planner that gathers useful trip context before opening contact.
- Mobile-first navigation and sticky inquiry affordance.
- Source links for material factual claims and review excerpts.

## Patterns avoided

- Unverified prices, route distances, departure dates or wildlife guarantees.
- Fake review stars, fabricated traveler names and unsourced badges.
- Gold-on-black “luxury” styling, excessive rounding, gradients and animation.
- Thin placeholder pages and dead navigation links.
- AI-generated destination photography or generic stock hikers.
